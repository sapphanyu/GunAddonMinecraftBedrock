# Tactical Guns Domain

Domain vocabulary for the Tactical Guns Minecraft Bedrock add-on, focusing on firearm mechanics, projectile ballistics, and tactical weapon feel in Minecraft Bedrock.

## Language

**Tactical Guns**:
A Minecraft Bedrock Edition add-on introducing modern tactical firearms and gunplay mechanics into Minecraft.
_Avoid_: Gun mod, weapon script, server plugin

**Glock-18**:
A lightweight tactical machine pistol chambered in 9mm with full-automatic firing capabilities.
_Avoid_: Handgun, pistol-18, G18 mod

**Full-Automatic Fire**:
Continuous projectile discharge triggered by continuously holding the item use action without requiring repeated manual clicks.
_Avoid_: Burst fire, spam click, semi-auto

**High-Velocity Bullet Projectile**:
A custom Minecraft entity fired by firearms featuring near-zero gravitational drop and high velocity to approximate instantaneous hitscan ballistics.
_Avoid_: Arrow, snowball, raycast beam

**Extruded 2D Viewmodel**:
A classic Minecraft handheld rendering style where a 2D pixel art item texture is rendered with 3D volumetric extrusion in both first-person and third-person perspectives.
_Avoid_: 3D mesh, Blockbench geo model, flat sprite

**Tactical Namespace**:
The standardized identifier prefix `gun:` utilized across behavior and resource packs for weapons, entities, and definitions.
_Avoid_: weapon:, csgo:, val:
